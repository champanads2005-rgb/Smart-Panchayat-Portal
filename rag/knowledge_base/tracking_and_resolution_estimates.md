# Tracking Your Complaint and Resolution-Time Estimates

You can track any complaint you've submitted from the "Track Complaint"
page, which shows the current status and the full history of status
changes with timestamps.

If your complaint is still open (not Resolved or Rejected), the page
also shows an AI-estimated resolution time in days. This estimate is
based on the complaint's category, its priority, and how many similar
complaints have recently been reported in your village. It is
**an estimate, not a guaranteed deadline** — actual resolution time
depends on real-world factors the model doesn't know about, such as
officer availability, weather, or material supply. If the estimate was
produced by a model still trained on bootstrap/example data rather than
real historical resolutions, the portal marks it as a lower-confidence
estimate.
