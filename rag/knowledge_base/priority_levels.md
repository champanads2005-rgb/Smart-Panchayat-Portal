# Priority Levels

Complaints can have one of three priority levels: Low, Medium, or High.
Priority affects how urgently a complaint is expected to be handled by
Panchayat officers, but it does not change what category the complaint
belongs to.

When you fill in a complaint's description and category, the portal's
AI can suggest a priority level, along with a rough confidence score and
the factors that influenced the suggestion (for example, urgent wording
in your description, or a number of similar recent complaints already
reported in your village). This is only a suggestion — you must click
"Use this" for it to be applied, and you can always choose a different
priority yourself. Officers can also change a complaint's priority
during review.

The AI priority model is a genuinely trained classifier, but it was
bootstrapped on synthetic example data because the portal had no real
historical complaint history to learn from at the time it was built. Its
accuracy on real citizen complaints has not yet been separately measured.
