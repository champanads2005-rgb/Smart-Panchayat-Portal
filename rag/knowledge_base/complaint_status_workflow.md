# Complaint Status Workflow

Every complaint moves through a status field that can be one of:
Submitted, Under Review, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved, or Rejected.

- Submitted: the complaint has been received by the portal. This is the
  automatic starting status the moment you submit the form.
- Under Review: a Panchayat officer or admin is looking into the
  complaint to verify details before acting on it.
- Assigned: the complaint has been assigned to a specific officer or
  department for action.
- In Progress: work on resolving the complaint has started.
- Resolved: the issue has been fixed and the complaint is closed.
- Rejected: the complaint was reviewed and could not be acted on (for
  example, insufficient information, or it falls outside Panchayat
  jurisdiction).

Every status change is recorded with a timestamp in the complaint's
tracking history, which you can see on the "Track Complaint" page. Only
an officer or admin can change a complaint's status.
