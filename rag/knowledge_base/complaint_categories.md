# Complaint Categories

The Smart Panchayat Portal accepts complaints in the following
categories: Water, Electricity, Road, Sanitation, Street Light,
Drainage, and Other.

- Water: drinking water supply, pipeline leaks, water quality, borewell
  and tank issues.
- Electricity: power cuts, transformer problems, damaged poles, exposed
  wiring, meter issues.
- Road: potholes, road damage, missing dividers, missing speed breakers.
- Sanitation: uncollected garbage, public toilet maintenance, sewage
  overflow, waste management.
- Street Light: broken or missing street lights.
- Drainage: blocked or overflowing drains, stagnant water, missing
  drain covers.
- Other: anything that does not fit the above categories, such as stray
  animal issues, community facility requests, or noise complaints.

When you submit a complaint, the portal's AI classifier suggests a
category based on your description, but you can always pick a different
category yourself before submitting. The AI suggestion is optional and
never applied automatically.
