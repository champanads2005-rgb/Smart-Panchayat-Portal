# Escalation and Contact

If you are not satisfied with how your complaint has been handled, the
best first step is to check its current status and history on the
"Track Complaint" page — an officer may have already left a status
update explaining what's happening.

This version of the portal does not have a separate automated
escalation queue for reopening or disputing a Resolved or Rejected
complaint. If you believe a complaint was closed incorrectly or needs
further attention, contact your local Panchayat office directly through
their normal channels, outside this portal, and reference your
complaint ID.

The AI assistant on this portal can help you understand how the
complaint system works, but it cannot change a complaint's status,
reopen a closed complaint, or contact an officer on your behalf.
