# Possibly Related or Duplicate Complaints

When an officer or admin opens a complaint for review, the portal shows
a list of other complaints in the same category that are textually
similar to it, based on an AI similarity score. This is meant to help
staff notice when several citizens are reporting the same underlying
issue (for example, the same broken street light reported by different
households).

This feature is informational only. The portal does **not**
automatically merge, delete, or close complaints it flags as similar —
an officer or admin must review the flagged complaints and decide
manually whether to link or act on them together. Submitting a
complaint that turns out to be similar to an existing one does not
prevent it from being tracked and resolved on its own.
