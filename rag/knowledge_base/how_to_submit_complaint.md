# How to Submit a Complaint

1. Log in to your citizen account and go to "Submit Complaint".
2. Enter a short title for your complaint.
3. Write a description of the problem. As you type, the portal's AI may
   suggest a category and a priority level based on your wording — these
   are optional suggestions you can accept or ignore.
4. Choose a category from the dropdown (Water, Electricity, Road,
   Sanitation, Street Light, Drainage, or Other).
5. Choose a priority level (Low, Medium, or High), either manually or by
   accepting the AI suggestion.
6. Click "Submit Complaint".

Once submitted, your complaint starts in "Submitted" status and you can
track its progress at any time from "Track Complaint" using your account.
You will also see an estimated resolution time there, which is an AI
estimate and not a guaranteed deadline.
